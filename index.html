Checklist

  • At least 12 characters
  • A lowercase letter (a-z)
  • An uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • A number (0-9)
  • A symbol (such as ! @ # $)
  • No common words or patterns

GOOD HABITS

Build a Stronger Password

🧠

Use a passphrase

Four or more random words, like a short sentence only you would remember, are long and easy to recall.

🚫

Never reuse passwords

If one site is hacked, reused passwords let attackers try the same one everywhere else.

🗝️

Use a password manager

A trusted password manager can create and remember long, unique passwords for you.

📱

Turn on 2-step login

Even a strong password is safer with a second check. Never share your OTP with anyone.
